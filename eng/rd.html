    Vovo Glasses

    An object of art born from fascination with two basic geometric forms. Coloured glass elements create various hues and an illusion of changing shapes.

    Materials: borosilicate, coloured glass · Design: Ewa Hiller

    Piano Table

    Designed around the ritual of extending the table. Made of solid wood with austere structure and clean lines — power of expression stems from simplicity.

    Materials: handcrafted solid wood · Design: Ewa Hiller

    Mood Mirror

    Three wall mirrors of different proportions and colours. The metal shelf embraces the mirror face, providing space for small everyday objects.

    Materials: coloured mirror, powder coated steel · Design: Dorothee Mainka

    Bowler Mug

    Designed for relaxed celebration of hot beverages. Made of specially developed unglazed porcelain — the matt surface makes it unique and will age beautifully.

    Materials: unglazed porcelain · Design: Ola Mirecka

    Cork Stool

    Shape inspired by the material it is made of. Eco-friendly, highly endurable and very light — cork still seems underexploited in furniture design.

    Materials: turned cork · Design: Tomasz Rygalik

    Dee Table

    The simple form of an aluminium d-shaped side table resonates its modern look. Corrosion and scratch resistant thanks to anodising.

    Materials: anodized aluminum · Design: Fabio Hendry & Martijn Rigters

    We are experienced in a wide array of materials and technologies.

    Our agency is dedicated to fostering a sustainable world through circular design practices and heightened environmental consciousness. Our goal is to actively contribute to the transition towards a circular economy, guiding businesses on the transformative journey from linear to circular models through purposeful design interventions.
